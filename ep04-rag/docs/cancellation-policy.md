# Cancellation policy (demo clinic, fictional)

You can cancel or move an appointment for free up to 24 hours before it starts.
Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment, including same-day
cancellations, are charged a fee of $50. If you miss an appointment without
cancelling, the fee is $100 and the next booking needs a deposit.
To cancel, use the booking assistant or call reception during opening hours.
Emergencies are exempt from the fee if you send a doctor's note within 3 days.
